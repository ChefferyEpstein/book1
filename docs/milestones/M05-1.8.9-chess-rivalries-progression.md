## Objective

Ship Chess as the next focused PvP game and make progression/prestige materially more rewarding without turning 1.8.9 into an unrelated feature pile.

## Delivered

### Chess & Rivalries
- Direct player challenges.
- Optional chip stakes with escrow and settlement.
- Separate Chess Elo starting at 1200, K=32.
- Server-authoritative board, move legality, turns, clocks, settlement and ratings.
- Time controls:
  - Bullet 1+0
  - Blitz 3+2
  - Rapid 10+0
  - Untimed
- Legal chess support includes:
  - check / checkmate
  - stalemate
  - castling
  - en passant
  - promotion / underpromotion
  - resignation
  - draw offer / accept / decline
  - timeout claims
- Move history and recent-game history.
- Chess contributes to player XP.
- Weekly challenge pool now includes **Checkmate** — win 1 Chess game → 5,000 chips + 500 XP.

### Scalable levels
The old flat 500 XP-per-level model is gone.

- Level 1 requires **100 XP**.
- Requirements rise progressively across 25 levels.
- Level 25 requires **1,500 XP**.
- A full prestige now requires exactly **20,000 XP**.
- Existing prestige counts and XP offsets are preserved.

### Prestige rewards
Prestige now changes gameplay rather than just the badge.

Free Spin cooldown:
- P0 — 30 min
- P1 — 25 min
- P2 — 20 min
- P3+ — 15 min
- P5+ — 10 min
- P10+ — 5 min

The reward ladder is isolated so future prestige-exclusive games/perks can be added without changing the level system.

### Free Spin reveal integrity
The wheel no longer credits chips before the animation lands.

Flow is now:
1. server chooses the result
2. award is held as a pending spin
3. wheel animation runs
4. chip balance is credited only when the wheel lands
5. unfinished spins can be resumed after reload

This prevents the balance / realtime refresh from spoiling the result early.
