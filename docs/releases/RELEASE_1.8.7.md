# BOOK1 v1.8.7 — THE MEATBAG EXPANSION

**Apparently Connect 4 was not enough enrichment for the enclosure, so the regime has installed several more ways for you lot to compete, gamble imaginary currency and develop completely unjustified status.**

## Correct-score betting
- Match Betting now has a **Correct Score** add-on.
- Pick the exact race-to-8 result on somebody else's match.
- Correct-score bets are singles only.
- Odds range from **20.00 for 8-0** down to **6.00 for 8-7**.
- As with normal betting, you cannot bet on your own match and the market shuts when score reporting begins.

## Levels, XP & Prestige
- Book1 now has actual progression because apparently simply winning at pool was insufficient.
- **25 levels per prestige.**
- Earn XP from league matches, wins, positive ball difference and PvP games.
- Hit Level 25 and you can **Prestige**, resetting the level bar and adding a permanent prestige count.
- There is now a progression leaderboard so you can quantify exactly how terminally involved everyone has become.

## Weekly challenges
Three challenges now refresh with each game week:
- **Hat-trick:** win 3 league games → **10,000 chips**
- **Clock in:** complete 4 league games → **5,000 chips**
- **Run it up:** reach +10 ball difference → **7,500 chips**

Finish one, claim the chips. The database checks your homework.

## Bounties
- You can now put chips on somebody **to beat somebody else at pool**.
- Pick an untouched league fixture, nominate the player who needs to win and fund the bounty.
- Bounties range from **1,000 to 100,000 chips**.
- If your nominated player wins, **they** receive the bounty.
- If they fail, your escrow comes back.
- You can cancel before score reporting begins.

Weaponised encouragement has arrived.

## Fantasy
Every game week you can build a four-person fantasy team from the Master standings:

- **1 Elite** — Master positions 1–4
- **1 Challenger** — positions 5–8
- **2 Mongs** — everybody else

The tiers are frozen before the game week starts.

Fantasy scoring:
- Elite win: **10 pts**
- Challenger win: **12 pts**
- Mong win: **15 pts**
- Ball difference: **±2 pts per ball**

Yes, negative ball difference can drag your team backwards. Pick better meat.

Teams lock when the first league result lands. The weekly fantasy winner gets **50,000 chips**. A tie splits the pot.

## Battleships
- Ships can no longer merge into one enormous naval abomination.
- Fleets now require a **one-square gap between different ships**, including diagonally.
- Manual placement, auto-placement and the server all enforce the same rule.

## Under the bonnet
- Book1 is now **v1.8.7**.
- Database version is now **v29**.
- New markets, bounties, challenge rewards, fantasy picks and prestige actions are server validated.

**Six roadmap items in one drop. The software mines remain operational. Play your fucking games.**
